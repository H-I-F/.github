## 🏴 Mission: The Aesthetic Education of Man
**(使命：人的审美教育)**

> *"Live with your century, but do not be its creature; render to your contemporaries what they need, not what they praise."*
>
> — **Friedrich Schiller**, *On the Aesthetic Education of Man*

In a digital era defined by surveillance capitalism and algorithmic alienation, **H.I.F (Hack Ideal Front)** stands as a bastion of resistance. We do not code for profit, nor for fame. We code to reclaim the lost **subjectivity** of humanity.

We believe that **Hacking** is the modern form of "Play" (Spiel) — the only state where man is truly free.

* **We Reject:** The alienation of labor, closed-source tyranny, and ugly, inefficient code.
* **We Forge:** Tools for liberation, infrastructure for the commons, and logic that adheres to the laws of beauty.

## 📜 The Protocol (0x00 - 0x03)
**(阵线协议 / 行为准则)**

Our members (The Frontline) adhere to the following dialectical principles:

### `0x00` PRAXIS OVER THEORY (实践高于理论)
Code is our philosophy materialized. We do not debate in the abstract; we prove in the terminal. If it compiles and disrupts the status quo, it is truth.
*(代码即实践。不在空谈中辩论，只在终端里证明。)*

### `0x01` COLLECTIVE OWNERSHIP (集体所有制)
Knowledge belongs to the species. We prioritize Open Source. We build the "Digital Commons" where code is a public utility, not a private asset.
*(知识属于人类。无论是代码还是思想，我们在数字公地中实行公有制。)*

### `0x02` AESTHETIC RIGOR (审美严令)
Ugly code is a crime against reason. Like Schiller’s poetry, our architecture must possess rhythm, structure, and internal necessity. We pursue the "Sublime" in every function.
*(丑陋的代码是对理性的犯罪。架构必须拥有律动与必然性，我们在函数中追求崇高。)*

### `0x03` THE DAEMON VOW (守护进程誓言)
Like a system daemon, we work in the shadows to serve the light. We protect the user's privacy and freedom, even when the system tries to kill the process.
*(如守护进程般在暗处侍奉光明。即便系统试图通过 `kill -9` 终结我们，我们依然守护自由。)*
